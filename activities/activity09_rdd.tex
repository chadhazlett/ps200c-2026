%%% SETUP for Q1 %%%
\begin{framed}
\noindent\textbf{Setup for Q1-Q3.}  In the United States, Medicare provides nearly universal health insurance to people aged 65 and older.  At age 65 a person becomes eligible for Medicare regardless of their previous insurance status.  A researcher wants to estimate the effect of Medicare on hospital utilization, using age as the running variable.
\end{framed}

\vspace{6pt}

%%% QUESTION 1 %%%
\noindent\textbf{Question 1: Identify the pieces.}  Write down what the running variable $X_i$, the cutoff $c$, the treatment $D_i$, and a candidate outcome $Y_i$ are in this design.  Then state, in one sentence, what the continuity assumption requires here.

\vspace{6cm}

%%% QUESTION 2 %%%
\noindent\textbf{Question 2: What else changes at the cutoff?}  The biggest threat to RD is another treatment at the same threshold (``bundled treatments'').  Name \emph{at least one} other thing that changes at age 65 that could plausibly affect hospital utilization, and explain in a sentence why it is a problem for the RD interpretation.

%%% QUESTION 3 (Fuzzy RD on Medicare) %%%
\noindent\textbf{Question 3: A fuzzy twist on Medicare-at-65.}  In practice the age-65 cutoff doesn't \emph{determine} Medicare enrollment perfectly --- a few people are already on Medicare before 65 (via disability), and a few don't enroll right at 65.  Suppose the researcher finds:

\medskip
\begin{itemize}[itemsep=0.2em, topsep=2pt]
\item Just \emph{above} age 65, hospital days per person per year are \textbf{0.45 days} higher than just below (the reduced-form / ITT effect of crossing the cutoff).
\item At the same cutoff, Medicare enrollment jumps from \textbf{5\%} (just below) to \textbf{95\%} (just above).
\end{itemize}

\medskip
\begin{enumerate}[label=(\alph*), itemsep=0.5em]
\item Compute the fuzzy-RD estimate of the effect of \emph{being enrolled in Medicare} on hospital days per person per year.

\vspace{1.8cm}

\item For whom is this the treatment effect?  Describe the compliers in one sentence.

\vspace{1.8cm}


\end{enumerate}
